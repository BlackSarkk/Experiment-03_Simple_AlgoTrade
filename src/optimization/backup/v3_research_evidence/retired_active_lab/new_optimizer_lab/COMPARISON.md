## Verdict

`Best historical result source: the recovered C158 recipe — core_15m_long_optimizer.py (Stage-1 400 / Stage-2 800) -> stage3 helpers -> risk_policy_search_t53.py (200) -> campaign_2y_15m.py scenario 4 (300 strategy+risk, then 150 Bollinger). It is the only one of the three that produced Candidate #158, and the only one reproduced bit-exactly.`

`Most scientifically reliable architecture: the recovered C158 recipe, on four counts the others lack — sizing-neutral strategy search with risk frozen, warmup prefixed to every partition before indicators are computed, a real robustness stage, and a holdout that no search stage reads (asserted, not assumed). Its own weaknesses are known and fixable: drop the incumbent enqueue, rebalance the 0.70 TRAIN weighting, and keep VALID out of the objective.`

`New optimizer attempt: abandon. Not "repair": deep_15m_optimizer.py is structurally unfixable without a rewrite (holdout used for selection AND for risk optimisation, plus an EMA ceiling that excludes the known best region and n_jobs=4 defeating its own seed), and multi_tf_optimizer.py is a strictly worse ancestor of code we now hold in full and have proven reproducible. Every idea worth keeping from either — separate risk stage, perturbation testing, regime testing — already survives in the recovered recipe in a stronger form. Keep both files read-only as historical reference and as the provenance record for the fork; run neither.`

`Recommended next experiment: no new optimizer run yet. First close the two open items that do not need one — (a) the forward protocol already prepared for the five frozen challengers plus the C158+Bollinger shadow, starting 2026-08-18, which is the only uncontaminated evidence available; and (b) if a fresh search is wanted afterwards, build it from the recovered recipe's architecture with the boundary-saturation fixes already identified (widen rsi_period, volume_sma_period, risk_reward_ratio, volume_mult, atr_period, swing_lookback, ema_period), unseeded, on a fresh 60/20/20 split with a holdout sealed from day one — not by repairing either engine in this lab.`
